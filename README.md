# CleanMargin

Monthly contract profitability and repricing for independent commercial cleaning businesses.

**Status:** Initial development. The working application and validation report are being added. Live billing is not enabled.

## Product
- Contract assumptions and monthly cost snapshots
- Portfolio margin review, including missing-data states
- CSV cost import and exports
- Repricing scenarios and client review drafts
- Private, authenticated workspaces

## Commercial approach
The initial price hypothesis is $49 per month. Validate it with paid pilots before spending on acquisition. Revenue and competitive success are not guaranteed.

## Halal operating constraints
Charge a disclosed fee for useful software serving permissible businesses. No interest, lending, gambling, deceptive claims, or sale of customer data. No claim of religious certification.
