# Operating principles

This is a software-business policy shaped by the owner's requested constraints. It is not a fatwa, certification, or claim of universal scholarly agreement.

- Sell access to useful, described software at a disclosed fee.
- Serve permissible business activities. Do not knowingly specialize in gambling, pornography, intoxicant sales, or interest-based lending.
- No interest income, lending, debt trading, betting, speculative prizes, or late-payment interest.
- No fabricated reviews, fake scarcity, guaranteed-income claims, or exaggerated savings.
- Disclose recurring billing before consent. Provide a straightforward way to stop future renewal. No cancellation penalty.
- Do not auto-apply a client's price increase. Changes require consent and the existing service agreement must be respected.
- Do not monetize customer data by selling it. Collect only the information needed for the workflow.
- Do not encourage underpayment, unpaid required labor, misleading invoices, or omission of real costs.
- Avoid presenting payment-processor availability as religious approval. Review the actual processor contract and business arrangements with a qualified adviser if needed.
- Reassess new revenue streams and features against these principles before launching them.

Proposed pricing is a fixed software subscription, not a share of speculative future gains.
